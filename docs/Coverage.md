## Entering a tabletop result

1. Open the pending **Tabletop procedure**. For an Event, select the side used. For a guided bot Command, select the final eligibility box.
2. Add each changed space and enter its final counts. Use the marker panels for Prepared, Terror, Personality, Capabilities, and News. For a move, enter both the origin and destination.
3. Update Resources, Vassalage, the Political Display, prisoners, and Out of Play boxes where they changed. The app derives Available pieces and Control.
4. Preview and compare the report with the physical result. Apply once. The app records the eligibility box and advances to the next step.
5. Reopen an unfinished procedure from the saved campaign. Use the optional note to record physical dice. The app's seeded stream drives later automated steps.
